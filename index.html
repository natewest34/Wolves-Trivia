How it works

  • 10 multiple-choice questions a day — everyone gets the same set.
  • You've got 10 seconds per question. No pick when time's up counts as wrong, so no looking answers up.
  • One attempt per day — once you submit, that's your score. (Someone can reset your attempt from the Today tab if something goes wrong.)
  • The board for today unlocks once you've played, so you can't peek before answering.
  • Ties go to whoever finished faster — so don't dawdle, but don't rush into wrong guesses either.
  • Check All-Time, This Month, Categories, and Trophy Case for the bigger picture.
